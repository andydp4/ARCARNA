# How arcarna records its own use: a notice for staff

*Draft for the owner to check and publish (v1.2 Phase 8). It covers the
"Problem?" button, arcarna's own usage record and Friction Truths. Fill in the
parts in square brackets before you share it.*

## Why we do this

We want to find the parts of arcarna that slow you down, confuse you or go
wrong, so we can fix them. To do that, arcarna keeps a record of how the
system itself is used. It is about the software, not about you.

## What is recorded

On every till, tablet and phone signed in to arcarna:

- **Which screen is open**, and for how long it is actively in use (there was
  a tap, click or key press in the last 30 seconds and the screen was
  visible). The screen is kept as its general name, for example "the till" or
  "a customer's page", never which customer.
- **The title of each message you see**, for example "Order failed". Names,
  numbers and amounts are cut out before anything is stored.
- **Calls that were slow or failed**: which part of arcarna was called, how
  long it took and whether it worked. This includes the sale itself.
- **Crashes**, and **how long a device was offline**.
- **The steps of a sale**: started, opened Pay, pressed Complete, placed or
  failed. Not what was sold or for how much.
- Each record carries **your role** (cashier, manager, admin or owner) and the
  **device's name** (for example "Till 2"), the app version and the time.

## What is never recorded

- What is on the screen, or anything you type.
- Money amounts, customer details or anyone's name.
- **Who you are.** The usage record does not store your name, your login or
  any other way to tell which member of staff did something.

## The "Problem?" button

When you press **Problem?** and send a report, it includes the screen, your
role, the device, the app version, whether it was online and how many sales
were waiting. Your note is optional; please do not type customer details (card
and phone numbers, emails and postcodes are removed automatically). Admins and
the owner see your role, not your name. Your login is kept with the report
only so arcarna can tell you "Thanks, fixed in version X" when it is fixed.

Pressing Problem?, or a crash, may also send the last minute or so of the
screen to our error service, Sentry, with all text hidden. Sentry is tagged
with role, screen and device, never your name.

## Who sees it

- **Friction Truths** (the usage record) is seen by the owner only.
- **The Problem? inbox** is seen by admins and the owner.
- It is **never used in staff reviews**, appraisals or discipline, and it is
  never joined to a named person.

## How long it is kept

- Individual usage events: **90 days**, then deleted automatically.
- Daily totals (for example "the till had 3 slow calls on Tuesday"): **24
  months**, then deleted automatically.
- Problem? reports: [until the owner deletes them / state a period].

## Screen recordings (only in announced windows)

arcarna does not record your screen as you work. If we ever need to watch how
a particular screen is used, it will only be:

- after Friction Truths has shown that screen is a problem, and after a review
  with an adviser;
- on the chosen screens only, for **at most 14 days**;
- with all text hidden; and
- announced: while it runs, those screens show **"Improvement study on this
  screen until <date>"**.
