Roles · who does what

Two roles, one system

Everyone signs into the same arcarna, but what you can do depends on your role. Throughout this manual, look for these tags: CASHIER MANAGER

RoleWhat they do in arcarna
CASHIER Starts and ends their own shift, creates orders at the till, takes payments, looks up customers.
MANAGER Everything a cashier does, plus: products and stock, customers, monitoring and amending orders, logging expenses, reading the Truths, and running end of day.
ADMIN / OWNER As manager, plus approving new users, settings, branding, locations and business-wide configuration.

Getting in

Signing in

  1. Open arcarna.viger.cloud in your browser — it works on tills, tablets and phones.
  2. Select Sign in and enter your email and password on the secure account page.
  3. First time here? A manager approves your account before you can see anything. Until they do, you'll see a pending approval screen — that is normal.
  4. Once you're in, you land on the Control Centre — the home screen below.
Manager — approving a new person New sign-ins appear under Settings → User access. Approve them, then set their role and location. They see no business data until you do.

The home screen

The Control Centre

This is the first screen after signing in — a calm, live answer to one question: how is the business doing right now? It refreshes on its own every 60 seconds.

What each part means

PartWhat it tells you
Today's numbers Revenue — money taken so far today. Transactions — how many orders. AOV (average order value) — the size of a typical sale. The two side boxes compare today with the same day last week and with this weekday's 12-month average, so a quiet Tuesday is judged against other Tuesdays.
Business Health Money in today, money in over 7 days, orders today and new customers this week. The Workers: healthy badge means background jobs (invoices, stock, understanding) are running normally.
Risk highlights What needs attention — e.g. dead stock (items that haven't sold in a long time). Managers should glance here daily.
Quick actions Shortcuts to the most common jobs — creating an order, adding a product, and so on.
