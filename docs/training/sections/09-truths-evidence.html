Truths and Evidence

The Truths Centre is where the figures tell you how the business is really doing, with the Evidence behind every number. This section covers Truths at a glance, the Evidence pages, Needs a look, Staff Performance, staff targets and Order Timing, and who can see each one.

Truths Centre · what it is for

Truths first, Evidence behind them

A Truth is a short, plain answer about the business: how sales are going, which products move, when you are busiest. Evidence is the detailed page behind it, with the rows that add up to the answer. Every figure says which window it covers, such as This week or Last 30 days, so a number is never read without knowing the period.

The Truths Centre is for MANAGER and above. Cashiers do not see it in their menu. A cashier's own figures live on My performance in the Operations Centre, which Section 2 explains.

Who can see what

RoleIn the Truths Centre
CASHIER Nothing. Their own figures and the targets behind their colours are on My performance (Section 2).
MANAGER Reads Truths at a glance and opens every page of Evidence. Sees Staff Performance and Order Timing for cashiers and themselves. Works the cashiers' queue in Needs a look. Reads the staff targets. Cannot export.
ADMIN As a manager, plus: sets the Truths at a glance layout, exports Evidence, sets staff targets, and sees Profit Truths, Would have flagged and the Problem? inbox. Sees everyone in Staff Performance and works the managers' queue in Needs a look too.
OWNER As an admin, plus Friction Truths and every queue in Needs a look, including the admins'.
Only admins export Managers can open and read any Evidence, but only admins and the owner can save it as a file. Every export is logged with who made it and when. If you need a copy for a meeting, ask an admin.

Truths Centre · the landing page

Truths at a glance

Choosing the Truths Centre opens Truths at a glance. It answers What should you know about your business right now? with a set of cards, called widgets. The whole business sees the same layout, chosen by an admin. The line under the title reads Your admin chose these widgets. Each one says which window it covers.

Until an admin changes it, the layout shows: Sales at a glance (revenue, orders, active customers and average order value), Revenue by day, Revenue by category, Payment methods, Orders by hour, Top products, New and returning customers, Top customers, Stock movement and the Evidence guide, a list of every piece of Evidence and what it shows.

  1. Open the menu, choose Truths Centre. Truths at a glance opens.
  2. Read each widget's window before its figures. A widget that has nothing to show says so, for example No sales in this window.
  3. To see the detail behind a widget, select Open on it. Its full page opens.
  4. If Ask arcarna is set up, the Ask arcarna button at the top right opens it here too (Section 10).

Changing the layout ADMIN

Only admins and the owner see Edit layout. What you save is what everyone sees, so change it with care. Widgets a viewer's role may not see, such as Profit Truths, are left out of their view entirely rather than shown locked.

  1. Select Edit layout. A bar reads Editing the org's layout.
  2. To add a widget, pick one under Add a widget (grouped as Truths, Evidence and Guides; admin-only ones say (admins only)), then select Add widget. A layout holds at most 40 widgets.
  3. Under each widget, choose its Size (Small (a third), Medium (half) or Large (full width)) and, where offered, its Window.
  4. Use Move up and Move down to order them, and Remove to take one away.
  5. Select Save layout, or Cancel to leave it as it was. You see Layout saved: Everyone in the org now sees this layout. Every save is logged.

Below the widgets, admins also have Full report export: choose a Window and a Format (CSV or PDF), then Download full report for revenue, orders, customers and stock over that window. Like every export, it is logged.

Truths Centre · the detail

Evidence

Evidence in the Truths Centre menu lists every Evidence page, grouped in four tiers: Tier 1 · Basic, Tier 2 · Operational Intelligence, Tier 3 · Customer Intelligence and Tier 4 · Advanced & Predictive. Each card shows:

On the cardWhat it means
Reference, such as ARC-T1-001The Evidence's fixed name. Useful when you tell someone which page you mean.
How often (DAILY, WEEKLY, MONTHLY…)The period it is designed for.
Title and purposeWhat it shows, in one line, for example Daily Sales Summary.
FormatsThe file types an admin can export it as.
Coming soonPlanned Evidence. The card is greyed out and does not open yet.
  1. Open the menu, choose Truths Centre, then Evidence.
  2. Select a card. The Evidence opens with its reference, tier, purpose and period at the top, the headline figures, then the table of rows.
  3. Where the page has dates, set them above the Evidence.
  4. Select All Evidence at the top left to go back to the list.

Exporting Evidence ADMIN

Admins see an Export button at the top of each Evidence page. Choose PDF, PNG image, JPEG image or, where offered, CSV data. The file matches the screen. arcarna records the export on the server first; if that fails, nothing is exported. Managers never see the button.

Operations Centre · exceptions to review

Needs a look

arcarna never stops a sale or a refund. Instead, anything unusual is recorded and lands in Needs a look (Operations Centre), so someone above the person involved can check it. It answers Which flagged sales, refunds, patterns and contact requests has nobody dealt with yet?

KindWhat puts it here
Price (shown under Sales)A sale line below its minimum price or below cost, with the reason the cashier gave at the till.
RefundA refund the refund rules pick out. Always: a refund on another cashier's sale, and any refund with the reason "Other". By default also: a cash refund over £50, and a refund 14 or more days after the sale. Admins set the amount and the days.
PatternA week in which one person had 3 or more such events and at least twice their usual, or was among the team's highest weeks. A pattern is a question, not a finding.

Whose items you see

You only see items about people below your role, and never your own. A manager works the Cashiers queue. Admins work the cashiers' and managers' queues; the owner sees every queue, including the admins'. Nobody reviews themselves.

Working through the list

  1. Open the menu, choose Operations Centre, then Needs a look. The line at the top shows how many items are unreviewed for over 7 days. Aim to keep it at nought.
  2. Narrow the list if you like: Queue (All my queues, or one queue with its open count), State (Open, Acknowledged, Explained, Escalated or All) and Kind (Everything, Sales, Refunds or Patterns). It opens on Open.
  3. Read the card: the kind, its state, the person's name, when it happened and a one-line summary.
  4. If it helps, add a line under Note (optional), such as what the cashier told you.
  5. Choose one: Acknowledged (seen, nothing more needed), Explained (there was a good reason) or Escalated (someone above you should look). Escalating tells the people above you.
  6. Changed your mind? Select Reopen on a reviewed item to put it back to Open. The card keeps who reviewed it, when, and the note.
When a cashier names you under "Manager agreed" If a cashier sells below the minimum and chooses Manager agreed with your name, you get a Signal (the bell) asking you to confirm. Select Yes, I agreed or No. A No is passed to the owner. The sale stands either way.
Admin — contact-details requests land here too Admins and the owner also see a Contact details requests list at the top of Needs a look, where a manager has asked for a customer's phone or email. Select Approve for 24 hours or Decline, and Revoke access to end a live approval early. Section 7 covers this in full. The refund rules and when below-minimum Signals go out are set under Signals and refund rules in Settings (Section 11).

Truths Centre · admins only

Would have flagged ADMIN

Would have flagged answers Which sales went below the minimum price or below cost? arcarna checks every till sale, manager edit, API order and voice draft, and records these quietly. No sale was stopped because of it. Website orders sell at the list price and are not checked.

It is for admins and the owner only, so a manager never reviews, or sees the absence of, flags about prices they set themselves.

  1. Open the menu, choose Truths Centre, then Would have flagged.
  2. Set From and To.
  3. Read the totals: Sales lines, Below cost, £ under list and £ under cost.
  4. By product lists the biggest £ under list first. By person shows who set the price: the person on the till, or the manager who edited the order.

Truths Centre · the team

Staff Performance

Staff Performance answers Who did what, and what did it bring in? It counts completed orders settled in the dates you pick, less personal use: the same orders as the sales Evidence, so the rows add up to the sales you took. It is listed in both the Truths Centre and the Finance Centre; it is the same page.

A manager sees cashiers and themselves. Admins and the owner see everyone. When people above your role are left out, a line says how many, and that they are not in the Total.

  1. Open the menu, choose Truths Centre, then Staff Performance. It opens on Last week.
  2. Choose Dates (Today, Yesterday, This week, Last week, Last 4 weeks, This month, Last month, or Custom with From and To). You can also filter by Location, Role, Fulfilment and Channel, and switch Show admin cover on or off.
  3. Read the four tiles at the top: Gross settled sales, Orders completed, Average order, and the earlier period it is Compared with.
  4. Choose a tab under People: Volume, Value, Quality, Benefit, Speed or Fairness. On a phone, each person is a card instead of a table row.
  5. Select a person to open their own page: their figures, an 8-week trend, and the Orders behind them.
TabWhat it shows
VolumeEach job counted on its own: Loaded, Prepared, Completed, Collected, Delivered, Dispatched. Solo is an order the same person loaded and completed. Still open is what they loaded that is not completed yet.
ValueSales completed and Value brought in. Value brought in is split like commission: all of it when solo, otherwise 90% to whoever completed it and 10% to whoever loaded it.
QualityWrong-item refunds, reopens, unready taps, refunds processed, deletes and completing others' orders.
BenefitNet benefit: margin contributed, less discount given, price-exception cost and personal use. It is not profit: wages, overheads and refunds are not in it.
SpeedCollection and delivery on time, first promise kept, stage times (the median and the slowest 10%), and how quickly they acknowledged what the board flagged for them (Alert to acknowledge).
FairnessRates that put part-timers on the same footing: jobs and value per active hour, value per day, refunds and wrong items per 10 orders, KPIs met and Badges.

The rows Admin cover (admins and the owner, counted but never ranked), Unattributed (nobody named, or an account since removed) and Total sit at the bottom. There is no ranking column, no bonus and no pay figure anywhere on the page.

Read per-person figures with care The figures are a guide. No pay decision is made from them alone. While per-person figures are new, the page shows a Provisional note with a date: they are being checked against the team totals until then, so do not act on them before it.

Truths Centre · what counts as on track

Staff targets

The colours on Staff Performance and on each person's My performance come from Staff targets. It answers What counts as on track? Everyone can read the targets behind their own colours; managers also have this page in the menu.

ColourWhat it means
GreenTarget met.
AmberClose.
RedNot close. For the first four weeks after targets are first set, nothing shows red.
GreyToo little data yet to judge.

Only rates and percentages carry a target, such as Collections ready on time, Customer named on the sale or Refunds per 10 orders. KPIs met is the number of greens out of the targets with enough data. Targets carry no pay. The In force card shows the current version and when it was set; if none has been set, every colour is grey.

Setting targets ADMIN

  1. Open Staff targets in the Truths Centre. Admins see Change the targets.
  2. Tick each measure you want to use, then fill in Green at least and Amber at least (they read at most for measures where lower is better). Data needed is how much data a person must have before they get a colour; leave it blank for the default.
  3. If you like, add Why the change (optional).
  4. Select Save as a new version. The old version stays in the History table and in the admin log.

Truths Centre · how fast orders move

Order Timing

Order Timing answers How fast do orders move, and where do they wait? On time means ready (for a collection) or handed over (for a delivery) by the promised time plus the grace. Choose Dates and Group by (Fulfilment, Assignee, Completer, Loader, Hour received, Day or Channel) to see where time is lost. Section 4 walks through the page in full.

As with Staff Performance, a manager sees cashiers and themselves when grouping by person, and people above your role are not listed and not in the summary. Per-person groupings carry a Provisional until … badge while they are new.

Ask instead of searching If Ask arcarna is set up, you can ask it things like "Who has the most unreviewed flags in Needs a look?" or "How did we do last Saturday vs the one before?". It reads the same Evidence, inside your role, and links to it under the answer. Section 10 explains.
