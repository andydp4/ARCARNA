Brand principles & guidelines

A short guide so anyone speaking for the business — on a receipt, an email, a sign or a social post — sounds and looks unmistakably like arcarna.

What we are

arcarna in one breath

arcarna helps independent businesses understand what's really happening inside them — and decide what to do next. It reveals the truth already hiding in the sales, stock, customers and costs. The promise is Reveal Your Truth™. We are a calm, experienced adviser, not a hype machine — we show the evidence and let the owner decide.

The one rule people break

The name is always lowercase

It is arcarna — lowercase, everywhere, always. At the start of a sentence, in a headline, in a legal footer, in an email. Never "Arcarna", never "ARCARNA". The only place the letters are drawn differently is the official logo artwork. This is the single easiest way to spot something that's off-brand.

How we sound

Voice & tone

Calm, plain, confident and led by evidence. Short sentences, one idea at a time. British English. We explain before we persuade, and we end with the next step where there is one. We never shout.

DoDon't
"Your margin fell from 33% to 28% last month. Here's why, and here's the next move.""Revolutionary AI that will transform your business overnight!"
State the number and its window: "down 7% vs last 30 days".Vague drama: "performing poorly".
Plain apology when something breaks, and what happens next.Blame the customer, the browser or the network.
Words we never use revolutionary · game-changing · AI-powered · world-class · magic · leverage · utilise · synergy. And never any emoji. If a sentence needs an exclamation mark to carry it, rewrite the sentence.

How we look

Colour

Our primary colour is Truth Blue (#3C7AC4). It means "this matters", so it's used sparingly — on the thing that deserves attention, never as wallpaper. When everything is blue, nothing stands out. Backgrounds lean to deep navy and clean off-white. We never use neon, and never purple as a primary colour. Colour always carries meaning; it is never decoration.

How we look

Type & the logo

Headlines and the wordmark are set in New Astro / New Astro Soft; running text is Inter; codes, prices and reference values use IBM Plex Mono.

There are four official logo versions only — master, reverse (for dark backgrounds), monochrome, and the app icon (the square). Always take them from the asset library. Never:

Never do this to the logo
Stretch, squash, rotate or crop it.
Recolour it or add gradients, gloss, shadows or outlines.
Rebuild the wordmark by typing it — the "a" is a drawn mark, not a keyboard letter.
Put the light version on a dark background, or the reverse version on a light one.
Crowd it — always leave clear space around it.

Before you publish

The arcarna test

Before anything goes out with the arcarna name on it — a receipt, an email, a sign, a post — ask these. If any answer is "no", it isn't finished.

  1. Does it reveal something true and useful?
  2. Is it honest — no exaggeration, nothing hidden?
  3. Is it calm and clear, and would a shop owner understand it without explanation?
  4. Is the name lowercase, the language ours, and the colour meaningful?
  5. Does it feel unmistakably like arcarna?
