arcarna helps independent business owners see what they couldn't see before. It is the instrument an operator trusts to tell them the truth about their business — and show them what to do about it.

## What arcarna does

Every screen exists to convert data the owner already has into a truth they can act on today.

- **Point of sale** — fast checkout on tills, tablets and phones. Cash, card and split payments.
- **Inventory** — stock levels by location, transfers, and low-stock signals before you run out.
- **Customers** — profiles, purchase history, and loyalty that rewards repeat business.
- **Truths** — evidence from your sales, stock and costs. Not generic dashboards — truths with next moves.
- **Control Centre** — daily business health at a glance. Revenue, margin, and what changed.

## The promise

> You will always know the truth of your business — and what to do next.

arcarna began with a number that didn't add up: a strong sales week that ended with only £300 left in the business. Revenue had been real. Profit had not. That gap is what arcarna exists to make impossible to miss.

## Voice and language

arcarna speaks calmly, plainly and with evidence. We say **Truths** not insights, **Control Centre** not dashboard, **Evidence** not reports. The name is always lowercase: **arcarna**.
