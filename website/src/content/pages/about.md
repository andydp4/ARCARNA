Viger Cloud is the home for business tools that help independent operators understand what's really happening in their business — and decide what to do next.

We build software that reveals truths already hiding in sales, stock, customers and costs. No hype, no dashboards that impress but don't inform. Just evidence, clarity and next moves.

## Our approach

Every product in the Viger Cloud suite shares the same principles:

- **Truth over flattery** — show the real number, even when it's uncomfortable.
- **Clarity over cleverness** — plain language and plain visuals.
- **Action over analysis** — a truth that doesn't lead to a next move is unfinished.
- **Calm over noise** — signal, never alarmism.

## The suite

Today, **arcarna** is live — point of sale, inventory, customers and the truths behind your numbers. More tools are on the way: Vault for backups, Sanctum for file storage, and specialist apps for email, receipts, invoicing and finance.

Each app runs on its own subdomain with a single shared login across viger.cloud.

## Who we serve

Independent retailers, hospitality operators and service businesses who run their own shops, tills and books. Owners who want an instrument they can trust — not another system to fight.
